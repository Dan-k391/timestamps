Keyboard and mouse statistics, a Statistics page you can rearrange, and a way to count time away as off.

### Install

**Windows:** from 1.1.0, use Settings → About → **Update**, or run the setup. Your data is kept either way. The setup isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

**macOS:** unzip, drag **Timestamps** into *Applications* and open it from there. It isn't notarized by Apple, so the first start is blocked: open it once, then *System Settings → Privacy & Security* → **Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 and earlier, Control-click the app → *Open*). It asks for Accessibility (window titles and documents), Screen Recording (screenshots) and, per browser, Automation (website addresses). The macOS version hasn't been tested on many Macs yet, so please report what doesn't work.

### What's new

- **Keyboard and mouse use**
  - Timestamps counts key presses, left, right, middle and other clicks, mouse distance and scrolling, like [KeyStats](https://github.com/debugtheworldbot/keyStats).
  - **Day view:** the totals update as you type, each application shows its keys and clicks, and a chart shows them by hour.
  - **Statistics:** totals, a chart by day of keys, clicks, mouse distance or scrolling, a keyboard heatmap, your top keys as a donut and as bars, and a sortable table per application. Daily totals can be exported to CSV or Excel.
  - **Privacy:** keys are kept as a total per key and day, never their order or what you typed. Keys pressed in an app set to Private are left out, except in the first second after switching to it. Nothing is counted while tracking is paused or marked off.
  - It's on by default on Windows, and can be switched off in Settings → Tracking. On a Mac it's off until you switch it on, and it needs the Input Monitoring permission. Mac counting hasn't been tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Statistics layout:** every block can be dragged, swapped and resized as in the Day view, with its own pin and reset buttons.
- **Time away**
  - **Count long away time as off** (Settings → Tracking): for a screen that never turns off, away time of *N* hours or more counts as if the computer was off. It applies to past days too and can be switched off again.
  - **Mark as off** (tray, or the power button next to Pause): records nothing until your next keyboard or mouse input, as if the computer was off.
  - The lock screen now counts as away at once, not only when its sign-in prompt opens.
  - Playing sound still keeps you active, but no longer brings you back once you're away.
- **Collapsed sidebar:** it now shows the tracking state, the app in use with a status dot, with Mark as off and pause in its menu.
- **Day ring:** the tray and title-bar ring now follow the clock, as the logo in the window does, and leave a gap until midnight so the day doesn't look over early.
